About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the School of Computer Science at the University of Leeds. My current research involves developing numerical testing software to investigate the numerical properties of matrix multipliers on GPUs.

I completed my PhD in April 2024 at the University of Strathclyde. My doctoral research focused on advanced polynomial matrix decomposition algorithms, including eigenvalue decomposition (EVD), singular value decomposition (SVD) and QR decomposition, with applications to broadband sensor arrays.

My broader research interests include numerical linear algebra, signal processing, algorithm development, algorithm optimisation, embedded systems and high-performance computing.

I have collaborated with researchers in signal processing and computational mathematics on polynomial matrix methods, space-time covariance estimation and broadband array processing.
