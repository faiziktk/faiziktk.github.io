01

About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the School of Computer Science at the University of Leeds, developing numerical testing software that reveals how matrix multipliers on GPUs actually compute.

I completed my PhD at the University of Strathclyde in April 2024. My doctoral work developed algorithms for polynomial matrix decompositions (eigenvalue, singular value and QR decompositions) with applications to broadband sensor arrays.

More broadly, I work on numerical linear algebra, signal processing, algorithm development and optimisation, embedded systems and high-performance computing. I have collaborated with researchers in signal processing and computational mathematics on polynomial matrix methods, space-time covariance estimation and broadband array processing.
